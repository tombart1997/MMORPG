# Concept-to-model rebuild

Your local generator at `C:\Users\Tom\Documents\2D_to_3D` produced the source geometry for this rebuild. All **59 original GLB files** were replaced and imported into Godot. The resulting catalogue contains **110 runtime model paths**, including **37 creature models**, **10 resident scenes**, and a new house, crate, bridge and concept sword.

Open the [offline model gallery](gallery.html) for front, side and three-quarter renders of every model. The gallery contains actual Godot captures, including representative run poses. The [concept book](../../concepts_docs/README.md) remains the source for the game's design topics and art direction.

## What was rebuilt

| Output | Implementation |

The new house and crate are used by the existing world builders. The bridge is imported and available for placement. |

The 59 reconstruction jobs and the 59 original model files are different inventories: 15 original paths are direct reconstruction targets, and 44 use derived replacements. The additional 51 paths are the creature/resident catalogue and four new assets. [Derived lineage](derived_lineage.json) records shared sources explicitly. Procedural terrain, collision shapes, water and effects remain generated by the game.

## Corrections made after opening the renders

- Reoriented reconstructed creatures so their heads agree with the runtime forward direction; regenerated six inadequate head references.
- Corrected the tree input mask after the first reconstruction lost its canopy. The final tree uses the transparent cutout.
- Fitted the horse and rider independently before inspecting their mounted pose. Projected the bridle onto the reconstructed head and connected its reins to the mounted grip.
- Removed small debris and painted floor fragments, separated low geometry between feet, constrained connected calves to one leg, and smoothed neighboring skin weights after run views exposed stretched triangles.
- Corrected the long-axis alignment of weapon variants so fitting preserved blade length and width.
- Partitioned the reconstructed fracture into the seven named ribs and core required by its damage/collapse animation.
- Fitted the house to the settlement's existing footprint and collision space. Preserved collision and interaction behavior for the replaced props.

Each primary model has its own JSON report in this folder with dimensions, reduction results, rig/clip details and applicable cleanup. Editable primary sources are in `blender/forge_rebuild/editable/`; fitted static variants are in `blender/forge_rebuild/derived/`. The full original GLB inventory is backed up locally in `.local/forge_rebuild/baseline/`.

The neural diffuse color was rebaked onto a 1024-pixel runtime atlas after mesh reduction. Godot receives those authored textures with approximate roughness/metallic settings; wisps also receive emission. TripoSR predicts base color, so these are **not independently predicted PBR maps**. The runtime preserves the reconstructed textures instead of replacing them with the previous character paint.

## Validation and visual limits

The [inventory audit](inventory_validation.json) verifies that all 59 original files changed, their bone/skin counts and animation names are preserved, and mesh buffers are finite with normalized skin weights. The Godot runtime audit loads all 110 scenes and samples their clips. Gameplay results are in [gameplay validation](gameplay_validation.json); real rendered townfolk, mounted presentation and combat reviews are in [rendered validation](rendered_validation.json).

The front/side/three-quarter sheets were opened and inspected, including the derived equipment/scenery and all resident scenes. Separate [horse/rider captures](../screenshots/horse-reference/godot/forge_rebuild/) cover the unmounted horse, separate rider and mounted pair. The static mounted review measured approximately **6.7 mm ankle error** and **0.7 mm rein-hand error**. The mounted attack review also checks those contacts while the hips and shoulders turn.

The [inspection record](visual_review.json) lists the 42 opened gallery sheets and their hashes. Additional captures show the [settlement](world_review/townfolk_crossing.png), [house roof and footprint](world_review/townfolk_roof.png), [wilderness](world_review/townfolk_wilderness.png), [mounted sword attack](world_review/mounted_cut0_frame15.png) and [creature combat](world_review/cairn_sentinel_impact.png). The [face close-up](world_review/hero_face.png) shows the reconstruction's current detail limit.

A separate rendered performance run at 1280×720, uncapped, on the GTX 1080 measured median frame intervals of **6.75 ms in the settlement** and **7.54 ms in the wilderness**, with wilderness p95 **16.53 ms** and 117 actors. [Full measurements](performance.json) include the frozen-enemy and disabled-shadow comparisons. These are measurements of this local scene and hardware.

These are playable **prototype reconstructions**. Close views still show soft faces, coarse fingers, clumped foliage, inferred back surfaces, thin armor overlays and uneven limb topology. Some mossling, stonekin and bandit poses retain pinching or compressed surfaces; the weight corrections reduce severe stretching but do not provide artist-retopologized anatomy. The current resident fallback is a full body rather than the previous interchangeable modular kit. Related weapon silhouettes and several residents need further art differentiation for a finished release. Passing the runtime checks does not certify final art quality.

## Repeat the import and review

Use Python 3.12, Blender and Godot available on this machine. From the project root:

```powershell
Godot_console.exe --headless --editor --path game --import
py -3.12 tools/forge_verify_inventory.py
py -3.12 tools/forge_validate_godot.py
py -3.12 tools/forge_render_reviews.py
py -3.12 tools/forge_render_all.py
py -3.12 tools/forge_render_all.py --legacy
py -3.12 tools/forge_render_all.py --residents
py -3.12 tools/forge_write_gallery.py
```

Import after changing a GLB and before reviewing it: standalone game runs may use cached imports. Keep generation/model writes and Godot import sequential. The saved plan is the build manifest; `initialize` starts a new plan and should not be used to inspect this completed build. The binder, derived-model exporter and skin-refinement scripts are available in `blender/scripts/` for deliberate model edits. Rebinding a raw candidate replaces later cleanup, so use the retained fitted editable source when making local refinements.
