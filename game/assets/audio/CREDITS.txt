Audio assets for Ironfield

Recorded foley: Kenney RPG Audio and Impact Sounds, CC0.
https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
See bundled license files.

Music: original Ironfield compositions generated by tools/build_game_audio.py.
Ambience and elemental pulses: original procedural audio from the same authoring script.
NPC voice recordings: synthetic voices generated from the project dialogue during authoring
using Microsoft Edge TTS through https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts .
These are synthetic voices, not recordings or impersonations of named actors.
All shipped audio plays offline; there is no runtime TTS or network dependency.

Horse vocal: Little neighing call #6, Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank, CC0.
https://bigsoundbank.com/little-neighing-call-6-s1217.html
A real outdoor horse recording, normalized and faded for the mount cue.
